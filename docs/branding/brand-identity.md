# Brand Identity

> Status: initial version from Product Owner input (Sprint 1). Refined iteratively as further decisions are made — see `docs/product/roadmap.md`.

## Personal brand, not a company

This is **Tommy Walid** — not "Tommy Walid Advisory," not a firm. There is no company name, no "team," no corporate suffix. Tommy is the product: the advisor clients choose to work with directly. This is a deliberate, guiding decision that applies sitewide:

- Copy speaks in the **first person** — "I," "me," "my" — never "we," "our," "the team," or a company name standing in for Tommy himself.
- The wordmark (`components/brand/logo.tsx`) reads "Tommy Walid" alone — no "Advisory" subtitle.
- The one exception is generic industry terminology ("investment advisory services" as a description of the *type* of work) — that's fine; what's not fine is anything implying a company entity separate from Tommy.
- Goal: visitors should feel they're talking directly to a person, not a corporate brand. Trust is built in a person.

## Brand Story

The practice is built around a single working principle: real estate decisions in Dubai should be made the way a family office makes them — through analysis, not through a sales pitch. Tommy lives and works in Dubai, and advises international investors directly, on the ground, rather than through remote intermediation.

## Brand Personality

- **Analytical, not salesy.** The starting point is data and long-term value, not inventory to move.
- **Discreet and premium.** Family-office register — warm, minimal, understated. No hype, no urgency tactics.
- **Educational.** Transparency is treated as a service in itself, not a compliance checkbox.
- **Long-term-minded.** The relationship outlasts the transaction — the advisory continues through rental, resale, and portfolio decisions.

## Brand Values

- **Conviction over volume.** Only opportunities Tommy genuinely believes create long-term value are recommended — not the full available inventory.
- **Relationships over transactions.** Success is measured by the durability of the relationship, not the number of deals closed.
- **Transparency and education** are central to the advisory process, not an afterthought.
- **Client peace of mind** is the explicit north star — in Tommy's own words: *"My objective is simple: my clients should sleep well, knowing their wealth is invested intelligently."*

## Brand Positioning Statement

For international investors considering Dubai real estate, Tommy Walid is the analysis-driven, on-the-ground advisor who manages the full lifecycle of an investment personally — not just the transaction — because most alternatives are structured around closing a sale rather than building a portfolio.

*(Operating context: Tommy advises under a RERA-licensed brokerage in Dubai; the brand is positioned as an advisory practice, not as an independently licensed brokerage. See `docs/technical/security.md` / compliance notes when formalized.)*
